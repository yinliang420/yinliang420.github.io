Science Advances 2026

Decoding active sites in high-entropy catalysts via attention-enhanced model

Identifying active sites in high-entropy catalysts is challenging because of their vast range of local atomic environments. We developed an attention-enhanced model to jointly predict oxygen evolution reaction (OER) overpotentials and dopant formation energies. We combined computational screening with automated synthesis and experimental validation. The workflow identified TiFeNiZn-CoOOH, which achieved an overpotential of 263 mV at 100 mA cm⁻² in 1 M KOH. Large-scale predictions and electronic structure analysis highlighted the role of Zn and the favorable [CoNiZn] local coordination environment. These findings provide guidance for the design of high-entropy electrocatalysts.

NeurIPS 2026 Accepted · Poster

MatCurvs: Article Real-Coordinate Curve Extraction for Agent-Ready Materials Reasoning

Scientific plots contain valuable quantitative evidence that is often unavailable as reusable numerical data. We introduce MatCurvs, a materials science data resource, and MatDeplot, a method for extracting individual curves and mapping them to the original axis coordinates. The workflow connects figure parsing, axis calibration, and curve extraction to produce structured data for computational analysis. MatCurvs includes 8,026 curves from 3,817 expert-reviewed plots. We evaluated their use in quantitative reasoning on 87 questions drawn from 26 single-curve plots. With the model held fixed, adding automatically extracted curves to image inputs reduced median relative error from 19.4% to 4.3%.
